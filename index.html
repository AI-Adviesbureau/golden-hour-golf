What's inside

Real golf. Low sun.

A golf game built for iPhone from the ground up, with honest ball physics, a course worth learning and the presentation of a Sunday broadcast.

Autumn Ridge

18 holes of autumn

A fictional parkland course built for this game: par 71, with a flyover before every new hole.

  • Elevated tees, doglegs, a par 3 across a valley and lakes in play
  • Greens with tiers, ridges and false fronts that reward a good read
  • Low morning or late-afternoon sun, long shadows and woodland on every hole

One-swipe swing

Know every swing

Pull down for power, sweep up through the line to strike. Your tempo and face angle shape the shot.

  • Ball physics with spin, wind, lie and slope decide the flight, the bounce and the roll
  • A shot tracer follows every ball
  • After each shot: ball speed, launch, spin, carry, apex and what your swipe did

Short game and putting

Land it. Read every break.

Console-depth touch around the greens, made for a thumb.

  • Pick bump and run, chip, pitch, flop or splash, and see the landing circle and the roll-out before you swing
  • Read greens with a colour slope map and grid, from a crouched read to overhead
  • A real cup: balls drop, lip out and horseshoe around the rim

Tournaments

Win it on the 18th

Play events of one to four rounds against a field of fictional players. The live leaderboard moves as the field plays around you, with a cut, a playoff and prize money. Grandstands, TV towers and gallery ropes fill up for the final round.

Career

From club golfer to tour pro

Create your golfer and start on the Local Circuit. Every step up is earned.

  • Climb four tiers: Local Circuit, Regional Series, National Tour and the Golden Hour Tour
  • Earn prize money and train seven skills, from power to green reading
  • Built for the long run, with seasons, promotion and a world ranking

TV-style presentation

Every hole-out on camera

Broadcast camera angles, a commentary team and crowds that react to your shots.

  • A cinematic flyover introduces each new hole; every hole-out gets its own shot
  • Four fictional voices call the action: lead commentator, analyst, on-course reporter and marshal (in English)
  • Tap to fast-forward the ball, double-tap to skip to the result

Game Center

One round. The whole world.

Every Monday a new weekly challenge: the same holes, wind and light for every player on Earth.

  • One official round per week counts from the first tee; practise as often as you like
  • Leaderboards for the week, your best front nine and your best 18, plus 21 achievements
  • Works offline: scores wait on your iPhone and go to Game Center when you are back online

Share your highlights

Hole-in-one? Show the world.

After an ace, an eagle, a chip-in or a long putt, tap Share and the game cuts a short broadcast-style clip, rendered on your iPhone.

  • 6 to 10 seconds with tracer, green camera, cup camera and a lower third
  • Vertical 9:16 for Stories, Reels and Shorts, or 16:9 landscape
  • Shared only through the iOS share sheet, only when you choose to
