Golden Hour Golf

Privacy policy

Short version: no personal data, no ads, no tracking and no account. The app counts a few anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

  • We do not collect personal data. No name, no account, no device identifiers, no location.
  • The app sends only anonymous usage statistics (for example "a round was completed") through Aptabase, hosted in the EU. Switch them off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no cookies and no account.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your own iPhone.
  • Game Center is optional and is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Highlight clips are made on your iPhone and only leave it when you share them yourself.

Who we are

Golden Hour Golf ("the app") is made by AI Adviesbureau B.V., a company in the Netherlands ("we", "us"). This policy covers the app on iPhone and this website.

Questions about privacy: info@ai-adviesbureau.nl.

What we collect

No personal data. The app does not send your name, contact details, device identifiers, location or diagnostics to us or to any other party we work with. It contains no advertising and no tracking code, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier. The online services of our game engine (Unity analytics, ads, crash and performance reporting) are switched off. The only thing the app sends is the anonymous usage statistics described below, and only while that setting is on.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library. The one exception is described under "Sharing highlight clips" below.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see how the game is played and where it can get better, the app sends a few anonymous usage statistics to Aptabase, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service. We use Aptabase's EU region: the data is processed and stored in the European Union (Germany). Aptabase processes it on our behalf.

What is counted, a handful of moments per session:

  • the app was started (with the difficulty level and the language of the game, and whether it is the first start);
  • the first finished hole, and every finished round (the number of holes, the course, the score relative to par and the mode: quick play, tournament, career, weekly challenge or multiplayer);
  • a career was started or reached a new stage, a tournament was started;
  • a multiplayer match was created or a turn was handed on;
  • a highlight clip was prepared for sharing (9:16 or 16:9);
  • the tips for new players were completed or skipped, and the difficulty level was changed in Settings.

With each event the app also sends the app version, the iOS version, the language setting of the device, whether it is a development build, and a random session number that is made new every time the app starts (and after an hour without events). On your iPhone the app only keeps two markers in its own settings, so it knows whether this is the first start and the first finished hole.

What is never sent: your name or your golfer's name, your Game Center nickname or ID, your Apple Account, contacts, location, photos, free text of any kind, the advertising identifier or any other device identifier. The app uses no cookies and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your IP address: like any internet connection, the request to Aptabase comes from your IP address. According to Aptabase, the IP address is used only for a moment to derive the country and region (never a city or an address) and a temporary anonymous code that changes every 24 hours, and is never stored. Aptabase keeps the statistics for up to five years. Because they cannot be linked to a person, neither we nor Aptabase can look up or delete the statistics of one player.

Why: our legitimate interest in improving the game (article 6(1)(f) GDPR), for example to see which modes are played and where new players stop. We do not sell the statistics or use them for advertising.

Turning it off: in the app go to Settings → Privacy and switch off Share anonymous usage statistics. From then on the app sends nothing to Aptabase. You can switch it back on at any time.

What stays on your iPhone

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your current round and scorecard, your career (golfer name and appearance you choose, prize money, skills, results) and running tournaments;
  • your weekly challenge round and any Game Center scores that are waiting to be sent;
  • your settings, such as language, sound levels, camera and display choices.

We cannot see this information. It is deleted when you delete the app. Like the data of any app, it can be part of your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), which you and Apple control.

Playing offline

The app does not need an internet connection to play. It connects to the internet only for Apple's own services described below and, while the setting is on, for the anonymous usage statistics.

Game Center (Apple)

The app supports Apple Game Center for the weekly challenge, leaderboards, achievements and challenges with friends. Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the app sends your scores and achievements to Game Center. Your Game Center nickname and scores can then be visible to other players, depending on your Game Center settings.

Game Center is a service of Apple. Apple's handling of this information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account, name or contact details through Game Center, and we do not run servers of our own for it.

Sharing highlight clips

After a great shot you can tap Share. The app then renders a short video clip on your iPhone and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination yourself, such as Messages, a social app or "Save Video". We never upload clips and we do not receive them.

If you choose to save a clip to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos. This lets the app add the video to your library; it does not let the app see or read your photos. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Temporary clip files are deleted after sharing and when the app starts. Once you share a clip with another app or service, that app's or service's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

The current version of the app has no in-app purchases. If we add optional purchases later (for example extra courses), payment will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we will update this policy before that happens.

Apple's own diagnostics and TestFlight

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymised crash reports and app usage statistics with us. This is Apple's service, you control it in iOS, and we only use these reports to fix problems.

If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send, together with the name and email address of your TestFlight invitation, with us. We use them only to improve the app and to answer you.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We do not use it for marketing, we do not share it, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for your question.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and loads no fonts or scripts from other services. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and protect the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The language you choose on the home page is remembered only in your own browser.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included: the anonymous usage statistics contain nothing that identifies a person.

Your rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you. Because we do not collect personal data through the app, we usually hold none: the usage statistics are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you (you can stop them in Settings → Privacy). If you have emailed us, you can ask us to see or delete that correspondence. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.
