Questions

Playing

How do I swing?

Put your right thumb in the swing zone on the right side of the screen. Pull straight down to set the power: the further you pull, the harder you hit. Then sweep up through the start line to strike.

A smooth upswing at the right tempo gives full ball speed. Drifting sideways opens or closes the club face, which curves the ball (fade, draw, slice or hook). After each shot the analysis panel shows what your swipe did. The short tour under How to play on the main menu walks you through it on the real screen.

How do I aim? (the aiming trackpad)

The left half of the screen works like a trackpad. Swipe left or right to turn your line, up or down to change the distance (on the green: the pace). Slow swipes make fine changes, fast swipes big ones. You do not need to touch the ball or the course.

Tap Scout to fly the camera over your landing spot; the trackpad works there too. To the hole resets your aim.

How does the short game work?

Close to the green the game picks a technique and club for you: putt, bump and run, chip, pitch, flop or, from a bunker, splash. The strip at the bottom left shows the other options with their carry and roll; tap one to switch. The game keeps the finishing point and moves the landing circle for you.

Before you swing you see the landing circle, the roll-out line and where the ball should stop.

How do I read and hit putts? (the putting slider)

On the green, the colour slope map and grid show where the ball will break. The slider along the left edge of the screen moves the camera smoothly from a crouched read, through the standard view, to straight overhead. You can also use two fingers, or the Behind, Read and Above buttons. The game remembers your favourite angle.

Aim and set the pace with the trackpad, then swipe to putt. You get one putt preview per putt that shows how the line breaks. In career mode you earn it through the green reading skill. If you prefer a permanent rolling line, switch on the putt line in Settings.

Can I speed up the ball flight or skip it?

Yes. Tap while the ball is in the air to fast-forward. Double-tap, or tap Skip, to jump straight to where the ball stops. This also works for putts and replays. Want it every time? Switch on Auto fast-forward in Settings.

Progress and Game Center

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically and only on your iPhone: your round, career and tournaments. If you close the app in the middle of a round, it welcomes you back and lets you continue. Career events and tournaments are never thrown away.

There is no online account, so progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes your progress. A full restore of an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) normally brings app data back with it.

How does Game Center and the weekly challenge work?

Sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. You do not have to; the whole game works without it.

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC a new weekly challenge starts: the same holes, wind and light for every player in the world. You get one official round per week, which counts from the first tee and must be finished within the week. You can practise under the same conditions as often as you like; practice rounds never count. There are also leaderboards for your best front nine and your best 18 holes on Autumn Ridge, and 21 achievements.

Offline? Your scores wait on your iPhone and are sent when Game Center is available again (a weekly score that misses its week expires). Challenging friends uses Apple's Game Center challenges and needs iOS 26.

How do I share a highlight clip?

After a hole-in-one, albatross, eagle, chip-in, a holed putt longer than 10 metres, a birdie on the final hole or a tournament win, a small Share button appears. You can also share during any replay, and the Highlights button in the round summary lists the latest highlights of your session.

Choose vertical (9:16, for Stories, Reels and Shorts) or landscape (16:9). The game renders a 6 to 10 second clip on your iPhone ("Preparing clip…") and opens the iOS share sheet. To keep it, choose Save Video: iOS asks once for permission to add to Photos. For now a clip can be made while you are still on the hole where the shot was played.

Settings and devices

Which languages are available?

English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Français, Español and 日本語. The game follows your iPhone's language. You can also choose a language just for this game in the iOS Settings app (Golden Hour Golf → Language), or in the game under Settings → Language. The TV commentary is in English.

I hear no sound. What can I do?

Golden Hour Golf follows your iPhone's silent mode (the Ring/Silent switch or the Action button), like most games. Turn silent mode off and turn up the volume.

The game plays nicely alongside your own music or podcasts: the game music stays quiet while other audio is playing. In Settings you can set Effects, Crowd and ambience, Commentary and Music separately to Off, Low, Mid or Full.

Which iPhones are supported?

Golden Hour Golf needs iOS 17 or later and is made for iPhone, in landscape. We recommend an iPhone 13 or newer. The game picks a quality preset for your chip automatically; iPhone 15 Pro and newer get the highest preset.

Phone getting warm? Lowering "Crowds and stands" in Settings to Light or Off reduces the load in tournaments.

Does the game cost anything? Are there ads?

Golden Hour Golf is a free download with the full Autumn Ridge course. There are no ads and no account, and the current version has no in-app purchases.

What happens with my data?

Nothing leaves your iPhone through us: we collect no data at all. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Beta testers

How do I report a bug from TestFlight?

The quickest way: take a screenshot in the game (side button and volume up). Tap the preview, then Share Beta Feedback, add a short description and send. Or open the TestFlight app, choose Golden Hour Golf and tap Send Beta Feedback.

If the game crashes, TestFlight asks whether you want to share the crash report; please say yes and describe what you were doing. Please mention your iPhone model. You can also email us at info@ai-adviesbureau.nl.
